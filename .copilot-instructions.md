# QSOL UFF development instructions

You are assisting with **QSOL UFF v4**, a transparent galaxy-dynamics and
compact-object research laboratory.

## Non-negotiable scientific boundaries

- Keep observational inputs, established baselines, phenomenological
  relations, and speculative research models explicitly labelled.
- Never describe a good rotation-curve fit as proof of dark matter, MOND, UFF,
  or an LQG model.
- Do not add LQG terms to galaxy likelihoods without a named effective metric,
  primary citation, classical limit, units, domain, and tests.
- Preserve SPARC's signed gas convention: `Vgas * abs(Vgas)`.
- Stellar mass-to-light ratios multiply `Vdisk**2` and `Vbulge**2`, not the
  velocities before squaring.
- Count every fitted nuisance parameter in information criteria.
- Relative AIC/BIC weights are candidate-set diagnostics, not posterior
  probabilities of physical truth.

## Architecture

- `uff/data.py`: validation and SPARC/canonical column mapping.
- `uff/models.py`: model equations and bounded parameter declarations.
- `uff/fitting.py`: likelihoods, optimization, and comparison statistics.
- `uff/sampling.py`: opt-in posterior chains; adaptation ends with burn-in.
- `uff/compact.py`: weak-field SMBH, Kerr scales, and LQG scale diagnostics.
- `uff/diagnostics.py`: plots and non-physical information/phase diagnostics.
- `uff/cli.py`: public commands and provenance outputs.
- `tests/`: analytic limits, synthetic recovery, and CLI behavior.

Keep `analyze_sparc.py` and `uff_model.py` as compatibility surfaces; new logic
belongs in the package.

## Required workflow for model changes

1. Write the equation, units, status, and citation in `docs/MODELS.md`.
2. Implement a uniquely named model; do not silently change another family.
3. Test at least two analytic limits and finite/non-negative behavior.
4. Add a synthetic-recovery or regression test.
5. Run `pytest` and the demo CLI.
6. Update `CHANGELOG.md` and user documentation.

## Engineering principles

- Python 3.10+, NumPy-first, vectorized, deterministic, and dimensionally
  explicit.
- Keep runtime dependencies limited to NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, and Matplotlib
  unless a new dependency has a documented scientific need.
- Reject invalid inputs early; never repair required measurements silently.
- Record every transformation and default in the summary JSON.
- Generated outputs, caches, datasets without redistribution permission, and
  local environments do not belong in git.

Project ethos: **Small is beautiful. Fast is holy. Transparent by design.**
